I‘m Yingao (Elaine) Yao, a CS PhD student at Cornell University advised by Saikat Dutta. I received my MASc in 2024 in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of British Columbia (UBC), where I worked with Karthik Pattabiraman and Caroline Lemieux. I received my B.Eng in 2021 from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China. Before PhD, I worked at Fortinet as a Security Researcher.

My research interests are at the intersection of Software Engineering and Machine Learning (ML). I am particularly interested in developing software testing techniques (e.g., fuzzing) to improve the security, reliability, and correctness of ML systems.
